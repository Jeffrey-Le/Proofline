How it works

  1. Add your resumePaste it once with a few facts like your graduation date. We keep only its bullet lines, for 24 hours.
  2. Paste a postingProofline finds who can apply, from graduation dates and enrollment to location and sponsorship, and every qualification it asks for.
  3. See where you standEach requirement is checked against your profile, and each qualification is matched to the resume line that shows it.
  4. Check the evidenceA match only counts if the quoted line is really on your resume. Click any requirement to see it highlighted in the posting.
Proofline gives a second opinion, not a final answer. Read the highlighted lines before deciding not to apply.Privacy
